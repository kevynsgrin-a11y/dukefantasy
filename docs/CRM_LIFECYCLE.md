# CRM lifecycle

Provider-neutral email states: anonymous → pending confirmation → confirmed preferences → active → unsubscribed/suppressed. Subscribe responses are non-enumerating and idempotent; confirmation/unsubscribe use signed expiring tokens.

Segments may include favorite team, weekly scoreboard, portal, playoff, coaching, and gameday. Odds/DFS requires separate consent. Welcome, weekly preview, Saturday morning, portal, and coaching templates remain drafts. No live campaign sends without explicit authorization.
