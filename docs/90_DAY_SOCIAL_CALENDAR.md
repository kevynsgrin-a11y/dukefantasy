# 90-day social calendar template

- Weeks 1–2: methodology, Clean Mode, source drawers, stadium-guide explainers.
- Preseason: roster continuity, fixture portal methodology, schedule utility, guide verification.
- Opening weeks: Friday preview, Saturday morning board, final-state recap, Monday corrections/model audit.
- Weekly cadence: portal verification, playoff scenario, coaching contract explainer, gameday logistics.
- Rankings/rivalry weeks: source-aware bubble paths and venue planning.
- Signing/portal windows: verified movement ledgers; no rumor amplification.
- Coaching changes: confirmed timeline and sourced contract math only.
- Bowls/playoff: season-rule explainer, reproducible scenarios, calibration review.
- Offseason: stadium, contracts, model education, and source-policy evergreen.

Dates and claims are filled only after the real calendar and sources are verified.
