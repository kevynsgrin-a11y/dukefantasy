# Consent and tracking

Strictly necessary device preferences may operate before optional consent where lawful. Analytics, advertising, and personalization categories remain off until applicable consent; no preselected boxes. Withdrawal is as easy as grant, and a versioned consent ledger supports audits.

The typed event layer excludes raw email, exact birth date, precise location, health data, and sensitive gambling selections. Feature experiments may not degrade accessibility, privacy, source transparency, or the commercial/editorial firewall.
