# Programmatic page rules

Index eligibility requires production-permitted data, stable identity/canonical, provenance/freshness, no unresolved incomplete state, and route-specific substantive modules.

- Team: identity/membership plus at least four useful modules.
- Player: identity/status plus at least two of stats, history, movement, or original profile.
- Coach: role/timeline/source history plus contract, performance, or carousel.
- Stadium: identity/address/date plus at least four logistics sections including accessibility.
- Game: stable event facts plus result/status and a useful preview, source, or logistics module.
- Collections: nonempty curated records, distinct explanation, and crawlable details.

Names-only pages, empty tables, source-free claims, and duplicated filters fail.
