# Source verification policy

Hierarchy:

1. Contracted authorized feed or original signed/governing document.
2. Direct institution, conference, venue, network, government, coach, or athlete statement.
3. Multiple reputable independently named reports.
4. Single secondary report.
5. Anonymous, user-submitted, synthetic, restricted, or rumored claim.

Statuses are `synthetic`, `unverified`, `single_source`, `corroborated`, `official`, `disputed`, `corrected`, `expired`, and `withdrawn`. Fixture demonstrations always use `synthetic`. Only tiers 1–2 normally support `official`; tier 3 may be `corroborated`. Tiers 4–5 stay attributed editorial claims or are omitted.

Scores conflict-check against an official result; portal events use licensed/public announcements, never restricted access; contracts require executed documents and second-person formula review; rules are season-versioned; injuries are never diagnosed or inferred; destinations require official ownership plus link checks; stadium facts receive seasonal verification; rumors never enter factual coaching fields.

Correction cases move `open → triaged → investigating → corrected/rejected/duplicate`. New versions preserve the old record and lineage, invalidate affected caches, and recompute derivatives. Frozen pregame predictions remain frozen. Identity, privacy, rights, injury, contract, and legal notices are hide-and-review.
