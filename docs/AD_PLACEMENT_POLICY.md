# Ad placement policy

No ad provider is active. Future `AdSlot` instances use a central registry, reserved dimensions, consent-aware loading, below-fold laziness, route/viewport density caps, and a global kill switch.

No autoplay/sticky video, disguised score/navigation/system card, mobile ad before utility, or ads during simulator steps and consent flows. Viewability and layout impact may be measured without unnecessary PII. `ads.txt` stays an empty template until a real publisher ID exists.
