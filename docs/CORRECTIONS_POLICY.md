# Corrections policy

Data-heavy pages provide “Report a data issue.” Cases preserve record/page IDs, accept public sources, rate-limit abuse, and avoid sensitive private material.

Material changes append a new version with source lineage, reason, date, reviewer, cache invalidation, and affected-derivative recomputation. Historical model outputs are never rewritten after outcomes. Identity, rights, privacy, injury, contract, and legal notices are quarantined first. Rejected or duplicate reports retain a bounded audit record.
