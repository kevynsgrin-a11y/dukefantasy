# Personas and jobs

Personas are behavioral hypotheses, not validated demographics.

| Archetype | Trigger | Minimum successful outcome | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saturday utility fan | “What is on and where?” | Status, kickoff, destination state, weather, next relevant game in seconds. | Utility buried, stale state hidden, or time unclear. |
| Program tracker | Roster movement changes | Arrivals/departures, continuity, impact, and confidence without a paywall. | Rumor or missing data presented as fact. |
| Scenario explorer | Playoff path debate | Force outcomes, rerun deterministically, inspect precision, share the same scenario. | Rules or seed cannot be reproduced. |
| Evidence-first researcher | Consequential claim | Inspect source, date, rights state, method, uncertainty, and correction action. | Opaque or mutable claim. |
| Gameday planner | Preparing to travel | Parking, transit, bags, visitor, accessibility, and last verification. | Expired policy shown without warning. |
| Opt-in DFS analyst | Deliberate fantasy research | Distribution and context after disclosure, without guarantees or operator pressure. | DFS leaks into Clean Mode. |
| Internal operator | Provider/correction event | Detect outage, staleness, rights state, and release blockers without exposing secrets. | Identity alone grants admin rights. |

One person may occupy multiple archetypes. Usability research must validate priority, language, and completion proxies.
